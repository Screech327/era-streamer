STEP 1 OF 3 · WELCOME

Welcome to ERA Streamer

This is the broadcast app for Rocket League — overlay, live stats, session recording, all in one place. Three quick steps to get you live.

First, make sure Rocket League is running. The app needs the in-game stats feed to push boost and score data to the overlay.

Checking…
STEP 2 OF 3 · OBS SETUP

Add the overlay to OBS

In OBS Studio, add a new Browser Source at 1920×1080 pointing to:

http://127.0.0.1:49124/match.html

It'll be transparent — drop it on top of your Rocket League scene.

STEP 3 OF 3 · YOU'RE READY

You're set

Pick teams, hit PUSH TO OVERLAY, and start streaming. Every match you play gets recorded automatically — at the end of the session click SAVE SESSION on the MAIN tab to keep it.

Closing the window keeps the bridge running in the system tray. Quit from the tray menu when you're really done.
